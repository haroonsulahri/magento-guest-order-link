# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, and assume good intent while staying clear about technical or security concerns.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and never posting customer data or credentials;
- keeping issue discussions focused on a reproducible Magento problem.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact options. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must not be disclosed in a public issue.

This code of conduct applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
